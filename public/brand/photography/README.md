# Photography — PLACEHOLDERS ONLY

Every `placeholder-*.jpg` here came from `05_Imagery (Inspirational)` in the
client asset drop. Those files have hash filenames, no credits and no releases.
They are **mood reference, not licensed photography.**

Use them to build and lay out sections. **Replace every one before launch.**

Art direction they stand in for, from the Brand Identity PDF (p.21-25):

- **Product** - tight crops of healed fine-line work. Natural light, shallow depth.
- **Lifestyle** - street-shot, motion-blurred, wide-angle.
- **Pop culture** - high-contrast black and white, rock-and-roll lineage.
- **Casting** - direct-to-camera, visible tattoos, minimal styling, diverse.

Constant across all four: desaturated or black and white, motion blur used
deliberately, tattoos visible but never the whole subject. Colour enters through
the brand overlay, never the photograph.
